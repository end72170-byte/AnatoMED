fetal ultrasound, cleft hard palate.
This image demonstrates the fetal hard palate as a horizontally oriented echogenic band extending across the roof of the oral cavity. The palatal surface is interrupted by a focal irregular hypoechoic cleft-like gap, with loss of normal smooth continuity.
The abnormal interruption of the palatal echogenic band is highly suggestive of cleft palate and indicates defective osseous continuity of the hard palate. Adjust the probe orientation as needed to better define the extent of the cleft, although the current appearance is already strongly diagnostic of a palatal cleft.
