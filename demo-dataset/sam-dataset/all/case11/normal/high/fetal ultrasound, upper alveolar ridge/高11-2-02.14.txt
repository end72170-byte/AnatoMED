fetal ultrasound, upper alveolar ridge.
This view is obtained by steady caudal translation from the orbital plane. The upper alveolar ridge is visualized as a bright crescent-shaped hyperechoic band parallel to the facial contour. Because the alveolar bone consists of dense cortical tissue with high acoustic impedance, it produces a clearly defined echogenic arc with posterior acoustic shadowing relative to surrounding soft tissues. The presence of a smooth, uninterrupted hyperechoic band is the key normal finding, confirming intact alveolar bone without fissure or defect and thereby excluding an alveolar cleft. Symmetrical hypoechoic regions within the arc represent developing maxillary tooth buds.
Continue translating the probe caudally while preserving stable alignment to obtain the mandibular alveolar ridge view.
