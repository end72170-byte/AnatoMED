This section is visualized after further caudal movement from the upper alveolar level. The mandibular alveolar ridge appears as a well-defined semicircular hyperechoic band due to its dense cortical structure and high acoustic impedance relative to adjacent mucosa, muscle, and amniotic fluid. The contour is regular and continuous, without focal depression or interruption, indicating intact mandibular alveolar bone without cleft formation. Small punctate hypoechoic areas within the arc correspond to early mandibular tooth buds.
After confirming both alveolar arches, hold the probe at this level and gently tilt the probe tip upward by approximately 10–15 degrees to direct the ultrasound beam into the oral cavity. As the beam passes through the oral fissure, allow it to extend posteriorly toward the maxilla, and fine-tune the probe angle to maintain perpendicular insonation of the palatal surface, thereby minimizing attenuation artifacts.
